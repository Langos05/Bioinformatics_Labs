The main problem with this algorithmic approach is its inability 
to handle the repetitive sequences present in real DNA. 
By using a greedy method that merges fragments based solely on the 
first text match it finds, the algorithm confuses different regions 
of the genome that coincidentally share the same code. This causes 
incorrect fragments to be joined together, generating a deformed 
final sequence full of errors and with a length that does not match 
the original genome.
